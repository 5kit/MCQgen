Q: A pump can fill a reservoir in 6 hours. After half the reservoir is filled, three more identical pumps are opened. What is the total time taken to fill the reservoir completely?
Category: Problem Solving (Rates & Work)
A: 3 hours 15 minutes
B: 3 hours 45 minutes
C: 4 hours
D: 4 hours 30 minutes
Answer: B
Explanation: The first pump fills half the reservoir in 3 hours. For the remaining half, four identical pumps work together. Since 1 pump takes 3 hours for half the reservoir, 4 pumps will take 3 hours / 4 = 45 minutes. Total time = 3 hours + 45 minutes = 3 hours 45 minutes.

Q: If $x$ and $y$ are positive integers, is $x + y$ an even number?
(1) $x^2 + y^2$ is even.
(2) $x \cdot y$ is odd.
Category: Data Sufficiency
A: Statement (1) ALONE is sufficient, but statement (2) alone is not sufficient.
B: Statement (2) ALONE is sufficient, but statement (1) alone is not sufficient.
C: BOTH statements TOGETHER are sufficient, but NEITHER statement ALONE is sufficient.
D: EACH statement ALONE is sufficient.
Answer: D
Explanation: From (1), $x^2 + y^2$ is even only if $x$ and $y$ are either both even or both odd, so $x + y$ must be even (Sufficient). From (2), $x \cdot y$ is odd only if both $x$ and $y$ are odd, which means $x + y$ is even (Sufficient). Each alone is sufficient.

Q: The average (arithmetic mean) weight of a group of 8 people increases by 2.5 kg when a new person replaces one of them who weighs 65 kg. What is the weight of the new person?
Category: Problem Solving (Averages)
A: 75 kg
B: 80 kg
C: 85 kg
D: 90 kg
Answer: C
Explanation: Total weight increase = $8 \times 2.5\text{ kg} = 20\text{ kg}$. The replacement must be 20 kg heavier than the person leaving. Weight = $65\text{ kg} + 20\text{ kg} = 85\text{ kg}$.

Q: A retail item's price is increased by 20% and subsequently discounted by 20%. The final price is what percentage of the original price?
Category: Problem Solving (Percentages)
A: 96%
B: 98%
C: 100%
D: 104%
Answer: A
Explanation: Let original price = 100. Price after 20% increase = $100 \times 1.20 = 120$. Price after 20% discount = $120 \times 0.80 = 96$. 96 is 96% of 100.

Q: How many distinct 4-letter arrangements can be formed using the letters in the word "CODE", using each letter at most once?
Category: Problem Solving (Combinatorics)
A: 12
B: 16
C: 24
D: 64
Answer: C
Explanation: The word "CODE" has 4 unique letters. The number of permutations of 4 distinct items is $4! = 4 \times 3 \times 2 \times 1 = 24$.

Q: A train traveling at a constant speed of 72 km/h passes a stationary pole in 10 seconds. What is the length of the train in meters?
Category: Problem Solving (Speed & Distance)
A: 150 m
B: 180 m
C: 200 m
D: 220 m
Answer: C
Explanation: Convert speed to m/s: $72 \times (5/18) = 20\text{ m/s}$. Distance (length of train) = $\text{Speed} \times \text{Time} = 20\text{ m/s} \times 10\text{ s} = 200\text{ meters}$.

Q: Is $x > 0$?
(1) $\vert{}x\vert{} = x$
(2) $x^3 > 0$
Category: Data Sufficiency
A: Statement (1) ALONE is sufficient, but statement (2) alone is not sufficient.
B: Statement (2) ALONE is sufficient, but statement (1) alone is not sufficient.
C: BOTH statements TOGETHER are sufficient, but NEITHER statement ALONE is sufficient.
D: EACH statement ALONE is sufficient.
Answer: B
Explanation: Statement (1) means $x \ge 0$ (could be $0$, so $x > 0$ is not strictly guaranteed; Not sufficient). Statement (2) means $x^3 > 0$, which is true if and only if $x > 0$ (Sufficient).

Q: A bag contains 4 red balls and 6 blue balls. If 2 balls are drawn at random without replacement, what is the probability that both balls are red?
Category: Problem Solving (Probability)
A: 2/15
B: 4/25
C: 2/10
D: 6/15
Answer: A
Explanation: Probability of first red = $4/10 = 2/5$. Probability of second red = $3/9 = 1/3$. Total probability = $(2/5) \times (1/3) = 2/15$.
